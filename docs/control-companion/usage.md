# Echo companion controls

The existing Control Panel has **Now & Next**, **Needs You**, and **Second Brain** links on Overview. Now & Next shows real work, recent results, pending decisions, and phone delivery status. Needs You opens its decision section. Saved active states after restart are labelled for inspection rather than claimed to be running.

Second Brain reads the existing local memory store in the current project scope plus global memories. Search, add notes/preferences/decisions/commitments, correct existing memories, or forget a memory and linked recall copies. Sources, confidence and privacy stay visible. Corrections and decisions use the version shown on screen; stale submissions are rejected. Typed drafts survive background refreshes and can be discarded explicitly.

Say **“send me updates to my phone”**, **“keep me updated on my phone”**, or **“I’m leaving”** to Echo on the Mac. The paired Phone receives important work transitions, results and questions for 24 hours. You can also use Start/Stop in Now & Next. Say **“stop sending updates to my phone”** to end delivery.

Open Echo Phone after connecting it to the Mac, then open **Missions → Updates from your Mac**. Tap **Enable alerts** to allow notifications on this installation. Updates are saved in the inbox even without notification permission. Questions have an Open Echo button to return to the existing Mac conversation and approval controls. Standalone Phone accounts do not receive Mac work.

The Mac must stay awake with Echo running. Delivery failures remain queued and retry with the same event ID, and the relay deduplicates notifications by installation. Private work is excluded; revoking Mac phone sessions ends the campaign. Device pairing and inbox data use the existing authenticated relay and durable encrypted account storage.
